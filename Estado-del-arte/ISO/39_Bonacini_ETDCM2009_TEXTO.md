DESIGN OF A NEW PROSTHETIC FOOT WHICH COMPLIES
WITH ISO 10328 AND ALLOWS HIGH PERFORMANCE

D. Bonacini a, B. Mangiante a, L. Vergani b, C. Colombo b
a Roadrunnerfoot Engineering srl
b Dipartimento di Meccanica, Politecnico di Milano
e-mail: roadrunnerfoot@libero.it, roadrunnerfoot.engineering@gmail.com, laura.vergani@polimi.it

Keywords: composite materials, carbon fiber prosthetic feet, testing ISO

Introduction
Dynamic elastic response prosthetic feet, made of carbon fiber, are available on the
market since the '70s. In the last years improvements in materials and manufacturing
technologies have allowed increased product performance.

Carbon fiber allows storing energy during the first stance phase and returning it
during the push-off phase. Prosthetic feet must therefore be highly dynamic to allow
a good roll-over and support the amputee in walking, but at the same time they must
be resistant enough to comply with the high standards of ISO 10328.

This standard consists of 3 kinds of tests:
- static tests: 1 static proof test at 1610 N both on heel and forefoot, and 1
  ultimate strength test at 2415 N on heel and forefoot;
- one cyclic test at 1330 N for 2×10^6 cycles;
- one static test in torsion at 50 Nm.

These loading conditions are 3 times higher than those during normal walking, while
the loading condition in the cyclic test is closer to the real condition.

To analyze the performance of a prosthetic foot, the hysteresis curve obtained from
deformation and load data is useful. The area between loading and unloading curves
represents the energy loss which does not aid the final push. Load-time and
deformation-time graphs identify the mechanical strength characteristics of a
prosthetic foot.

The Roadwalking foot has a completely new design, 4 laminates to support the user
and store/return energy during the whole stance phase, giving an effective
propulsive push-off.

The elasticity of this new foot does not make it weaker: FEM analysis was useful to
find a stratification providing both stiffness and elasticity. The Roadwalking foot
complies with the ISO standard and is CE marked.

**Relevancia directa para el proyecto:** valores de referencia de ensayo real de un pie protésico de fibra de carbono según ISO 10328 (prueba estática 1610 N, última 2415 N, cíclico 1330 N × 2×10⁶ ciclos, torsión 50 Nm). **Advertencia (ver Sección 6.1 de la revisión bibliográfica):** el paper no especifica a qué nivel de carga P (P3–P8) corresponden estos valores, y la razón prueba→última (1.5×) no coincide con el patrón 2× observado en los niveles P4 y P5 ya confirmados con fuente primaria — por lo tanto, no usar estos valores para dimensionar la celda de carga sin verificación adicional. Es más útil como ejemplo de protocolo de ensayo (curvas histéresis, carga-tiempo, ensayo cíclico) que como fuente numérica de niveles de carga.
